To find the total miles Sam ran this week, we need to sum up the miles he ran each day.

- On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, Sam ran 3 miles each day. So for these three days, the total is 3 * 3.
- On Tuesday and Thursday, Sam ran 5 miles each day. So for these two days, the total is 5 * 2.

Now, let's calculate the total miles:

- Total for Mon, Wed, Fri: 3 * 3 = 9 miles
- Total for Tue, Thu: 5 * 2 = 10 miles
