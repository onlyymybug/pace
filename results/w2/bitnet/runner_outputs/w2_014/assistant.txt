To find out how many carrots Mason eats in a week, we need to calculate the total number of carrots he eats from Monday to Sunday.

1. Calculate the number of carrots eaten from Monday to Friday:
   - 4 carrots per weekday
   - There are 5 weekdays (Monday to Friday)
   - Total carrots from Monday to Friday = 4 carrots/day * 5 days = 20 carrots

2. Calculate the number of carrots eaten on Saturday and Sunday:
   - 5 carrots per weekend day
   - There are 2 weekend days (Saturday and Sunday)
   - Total carrots on Saturday and Sunday = 5
