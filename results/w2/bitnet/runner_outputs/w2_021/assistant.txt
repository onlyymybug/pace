To find out how much money was withheld from Sally's wage, we need to subtract her total bank balance at the end of the week from her expected weekly wage.

Expected weekly wage = $300
Total bank balance at the end of the week = $420

Wage withheld = Total bank balance - Expected weekly wage
Wage withheld = $420 - $300
Wage withheld = $120

The answer is 120 dollars.
