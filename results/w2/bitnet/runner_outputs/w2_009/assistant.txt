To find the discounted cost, we need to calculate 30% of $100 and subtract it from the original price.

Step 1: Calculate 30% of $100.
30% of $100 = (30/100) * $100 = $30

Step 2: Subtract the discount from the original price.
$100 - $30 = $70

The grooming cost is $70. The answer is 70. In that final sentence, 70 must be a plain integer.
