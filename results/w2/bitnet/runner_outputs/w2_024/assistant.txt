To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of crabs each person has and then add them together.

1. Bo has 40 crabs.
2. Monic has 4 fewer crabs than Bo. So, Monic has \(40 - 4 = 36\) crabs.
3. Rani has ten more crabs than Monic. So, Rani has \(36 + 10 = 46\) crabs.

Now, we add the number of crabs each person has together:

\[40 + 36 + 46 = 122\]

The answer is 122.
