Problem: Kenny is selling his Pokemon cards to buy a ticket to an amusement park, which costs $100. He has a collection of cards and plans to sell them for $1.5 each. He keeps 1/3 of them and gets to go to the amusement park with $50 in spending cash. How many cards did he start with? Let x be the number of cards Kenny started with.
He sells 1.5x and keeps 1/3 of of that, which is 1/3 * 1.5x = 0.5x.
So, 1.5x - 0.5x = 1x cards are sold to Kenny.
He has $50 to spend, so 1.5x = 100 + 50.
Solving for x, we get 1.5x = 150, x = 100.
